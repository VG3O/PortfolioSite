About Me

Hello! I'm Brandon Salvietti. I am 21 years old, and my birthday is June 6th. I am a huge gearhead and car nerd, and this is what actually pushed me to learn programming. Around middle school is where I started, and it allowed me to make my automotive fantasies into the video game world, mainly through the Roblox platform.
